About Bonus Level Books

Bonus Level Books is an independent publisher of chapter books for kids who love video games. We publish 23 series so far, each with its own pen name, its own original cast and its own voice, from gentle read-alouds for six-year-olds to spooky folklore for brave teens.

Our books are unofficial. They are not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Mojang, Microsoft, Roblox Corporation or the creators of any game. Minecraft® is a trademark of Mojang Synergies AB. ROBLOX is a registered trademark of Roblox Corporation.

Privacy

This website does not use cookies, analytics, ads or tracking of any kind, and it does not collect personal information. Fonts are served from this site. Links to books take you to Amazon.com, where Amazon's own privacy policy applies.
