For parents

Lots of kids who won't pick up a book will happily talk about their favorite game for an hour. Our stories start there: worlds and rules they already know, with original characters, real stakes and short chapters that pull them to the next page.

Reading levels

Level 1: ages 6–8

Read-aloud and early chapter books. The Dewlight Fairies, Floor by Floor, Team Checkpoint.

Level 2: ages 8–12

Chapter books with real stakes. Bacon Hair Rises, Blockburst Academy, Brewstone Academy, The Extra Cheese Crew, Facility Breakout, The Gate of Eight Clocks, The Grand Lantern Hotel, Howlwood Pack, The Lighthouse Lookouts, Night Shift at Pawprint Hospital, The Nightshade Kids, Not a Peep at Granny's, Oddstone Middle School, The Redstone Detectives, Seaglass Mermaids, The Speedy Yolks, The Squires of Pebblecrown, Sunny Meadow Ranch, Tiny in a Giant House.

Level 3: ages 10–14

Scary reads for brave older kids. The Village of Borrowed Voices.

What's never in our books

No gore or graphic violence, no romance or crushes, no swearing and no cruelty. Game weapons are only used against monsters and hazards, never on people. The spookier series are scary in the campfire-story sense, and nobody is hurt on the page.

Games and online safety

Many of the stories happen inside a game the characters play with friends. When that happens, the good habits are part of the story: playing with friends they know in real life, keeping voice chat off, being a good sport and telling a grown-up when something feels wrong.

Extras at the back

Depending on the series, the back of the book has puzzles, code-breakers, solve-it-yourself case notes, quizzes and real-world facts about pets, horses, weather and history.

Questions

Are these official Minecraft or Roblox books? No. They are unofficial stories with original characters, written for kids who are fans. They are not affiliated with or endorsed by Mojang, Microsoft, Roblox Corporation or any game's creators.

Does my child need to play the game? No. Each book explains what a reader needs to know as the story goes.

Do the series have to be read in order? No. Every book is a complete adventure.

Where can I buy them? On Amazon, as Kindle ebooks and, for most titles, paperbacks.

Bonus Level Books is an independent publisher of chapter books for kids who love games. Each series has its own pen name and its own original cast.
