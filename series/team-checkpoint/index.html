Team Checkpoint

Spooky-silly chase stories for younger readers on the bounciest obby course in town.

by Bart Noonan

Ages 6–8FunnySpooky

For parents

You are the newest runner on the bounciest obstacle course in town, racing past silly traps while a dough monster gives chase. Short, spooky-silly chapters with a happy ending and a wink.

Format
Early chapter book: 10 to 12 short chapters in large print, written for about a 1st to 3rd grade reading level. Great for reading aloud.
How scary
Spooky-silly for young readers: cartoon chases and a happy ending.
Told by
Second person: the reader is the runner.
The big idea
Never give up: every fall is a do-over.
At the back of each book
Draw Your Own Obby and a Joke Corner.

A good fit for kids who are 6 to 8, love obstacle courses, jokes and just a little bit of spooky.
