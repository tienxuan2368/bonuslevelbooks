Escape Before the Dough Rises!

The dough is awake. And it wants to KEEP you.

It's your very first day as a runner at the Bouncy Crust, the bounciest course in Jumpington. The floor is sauce. The pizza boxes float. And at closing time, one spoonful too much Bounce Yeast wakes up the giant Pizza Day dough. His name is Kneady. The K is silent. Kneady is not. The Safety Lock shuts every door, and the only grown-up is stuck outside.
