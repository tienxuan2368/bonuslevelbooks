Granny Can Hear You Tiptoe

Every midnight, something in Granny's locked attic moans, clanks and gallops. And Granny can hear you tiptoe.

Juno, Fritz and their cousin Santi have five days at Gravelly House, the creakiest house on Tumbledown Hill. Granny Gravelly is very tall, very strict, and hears EVERY sound: a spoon dropped two floors down, a whisper under a blanket. Her rule is simple. Not a peep. At midnight, the attic above their beds comes alive with chains and hooves and thunder. The attic door has three locks, and Granny has the keys.
