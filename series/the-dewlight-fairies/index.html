The Dewlight Fairies

Gentle read-aloud stories about the smallest fairy in a mossy village, and the kindness that makes her brave.

by Posy Larkmoor

Ages 6–8Gentle

Book 1

Bluet and the Firefly Night

Bluet has waited seven whole summers for Firefly Night. Now it's going to rain.

Under the roots of an old azalea tree, in the soft green moss of the blocky world, lives a village of tiny fairies. Bluet is the smallest of them all. Tonight, she is finally old enough to fly to Plip Pond and watch the fireflies rise. But gray clouds are piling up over the hill. Wet wings are heavy, and in the rain, every fairy must fly home. And everyone knows you can't catch a firefly. So how do you bring Firefly Night home?

A Read-Aloud Fairy Chapter Book for Kids 6-8 About Kindness.

Book 2

Bluet and the Empty Fairy House

How do you say hello to someone who can't see you?

There's a new girl in the village up the hill. Her name is Ellie, and she has nobody to play with. At the bottom of her grandma's garden sits a tiny old fairy house that no fairy has ever moved into. Bluet and her fairy friends want to make Ellie feel welcome. But fairies stay hidden from big folk. And speedy Zaley, who makes friends by zooming straight at them, is about to learn that some friends run when you chase them.

A Read-Aloud Fairy Chapter Book for Kids 6-8 About Welcoming.
