Bluet and the Firefly Night

A Read-Aloud Fairy Chapter Book for Kids 6-8 About Kindness. Book 1 of The Dewlight Fairies.

Bluet has waited seven whole summers for Firefly Night. Now it's going to rain. Under the roots of an old azalea tree, in the soft green moss of the blocky world, lives a village of tiny fairies. Bluet is the smallest of them all. Tonight, she is finally old enough to fly to Plip Pond and watch the fireflies rise.

Bluet and the Empty Fairy House

A Read-Aloud Fairy Chapter Book for Kids 6-8 About Welcoming. Book 2 of The Dewlight Fairies.

How do you say hello to someone who can't see you? There's a new girl in the village up the hill. Her name is Ellie, and she has nobody to play with. At the bottom of her grandma's garden sits a tiny old fairy house that no fairy has ever moved into. Bluet and her fairy friends want to make Ellie feel welcome. But fairies stay hidden from big folk. And speedy Zaley, who makes friends by zooming straight at them, is about to learn that some friends run when you chase them.
