The Ninja Nobody Saw

Three kids in indigo and one sneezy baby panda do good deeds by night in a bamboo village, on stealth missions full of rooftop chases and quiet courage.

by Morgan Lisle

Ages 8–12FunnyAction

For parents

Nomi, Bash and Lu are the secret ninja clan of Bamboo Bend, with Thud, a baby panda who sneezes at the worst moments. Each book is a night-time stealth mission sent by a teacher they have never seen, told through the clan's list of rules, the Nevers, and short mission poems.

Format
Chapter book: 20 to 26 short chapters, written for about a 3rd to 4th grade reading level.
How scary
Adventure, not spooky: real danger and narrow escapes, but no horror.
Told by
Close third person through Nomi Hara, ten, in quick, quiet, physical sentences with a deadpan sense of humor.
The big idea
Quiet courage: do the good thing without needing the credit, and cover for your friends' noisy flaws.
At the back of each book
The New Never, a Real Ninja: Fact or Movie? quiz, a quiet noticing game or a write-your-own mission haiku page, and a short note for grown-ups.

A good fit for kids who love ninjas, rooftop chases and funny animal sidekicks.

Book 1

A Ninja Never Sneezes

A ninja never sneezes. Nobody told the panda.

In Bamboo Bend, good deeds happen at night, and the whole village argues about the mysterious ninja nobody ever sees. The truth is three kids in indigo and Thud, a baby panda who sneezes about every twenty-five seconds. Then a three-line poem from their secret teacher, who signs every note "Nobody," sends them to Flint Ridge. Something bright blue is locked in a cage at the pillager outpost, and it has to be home before the dawn bell.

A Sneaky-Funny Stealth-Mission Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.

Book 2

A Ninja Never Looks Down

A ninja never looks down. Bash wrote that rule himself. It never helped.

Bamboo Bend whispers about the ninja nobody ever sees. The truth is three kids in indigo and Thud, a baby panda with a very busy nose. Their secret teacher, who signs every note "Nobody," has built them a line of planks through the treetops of the cherry ridge. Nomi crosses it in a blink. Bash, who is afraid of heights, freezes halfway.

A Daring Stealth-Mission Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.
