Night Shift at Pawprint Hospital

Three friends work the night shift at an animal hospital game where some patients are not what they seem.

by Fern Wickersham

Ages 8–12SpookyAnimals

For parents

Dulce, Abe and Tavo work the night shift at a pet hospital in an online game, where some patients are not what they seem. Creepy, caring mysteries with no blood, no scary injured animals and real pet-care facts.

Format
Chapter book: 20 to 26 short chapters, written for about a 3rd to 4th grade reading level.
How scary
Spooky: creepy moments, suspense and real danger, but nobody is hurt on the page.
Told by
Third person, following one of the three friends, with the crew's shift log in between.
The big idea
Compassion: look closer, then ask what it needs.
At the back of each book
The Anomaly Files and Real Pet Care Facts.

A good fit for kids who love animals, vets and spooky-but-kind mysteries.

Book 1

Anomalies Need Checkups Too

The puppy on the front step looks like any other patient. But on the hospital camera, it's standing in a snowstorm.

Every night at seven, Dulce Castillo, her little brother Tavo and their best friend Abe clock in to Pawprint Hospital, their own private server in a night-shift animal-hospital game. Dulce works the Night Window, Abe runs the cameras and the flash, and Tavo names every patient in three seconds flat. The rules are simple. Check the tag. Check every patient three ways. And watch out for Lookalikes, the fakes that copy real patients. Let one inside, and the lights go out one by one.

A Sweet and Spooky Night-Shift Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.

Book 2

Anomalies Need Lullabies Too

At 1:33 AM, a tired little owl rings the bell. Every clock in the hospital stops.

Every night at seven, Tavo Castillo, his big sister Dulce and their best friend Abe clock in to Pawprint Hospital, their private server in a spooky night-shift animal-hospital game on Roblox. Dulce works the Night Window and Abe runs the cameras. Tavo is the Runner: he names every patient in three seconds flat and shuts every door.

A Spooky and Suspenseful Night-Shift Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.

KindlePaperback

Book 3

Anomalies Need Friends Too

At 10:10 PM, a kitten rings the bell. The camera says nobody is there.

Every night at seven, Abe Tesfaye and his best friends Dulce and Tavo clock in to Pawprint Hospital, their private server in a spooky night-shift animal-hospital game on Roblox. Dulce works the Night Window. Tavo runs and names every patient. Abe runs the cameras, and Abe never misses anything. Until the night he does.

A Creepy and Heartfelt Night-Shift Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.
