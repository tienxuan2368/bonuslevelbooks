My Horse Thinks She's a Unicorn

A Daring Horse Chapter Book for Girls Ages 8-12. Book 1 of Sunny Meadow Ranch.

My horse thinks she's a unicorn. My little sister agrees with her. I'm the one who cleans her hooves. Ten-year-old Tansy Mayfield lives on Sunny Meadow Ranch, where every sunflower faces east and every chore comes before every adventure. Then a wild white mare appears at the High Fence, and the whole village starts calling her the Sunny Meadow Unicorn.

My Horse Thinks She's a Snowplow

A Brave Winter Horse Chapter Book for Girls Ages 8-12. Book 2 of Sunny Meadow Ranch.

My horse thinks she's a snowplow. The snow doesn't agree. Neither do the things that come out of it at night. Snow never falls on Sunny Meadow Ranch, so ten-year-old Tansy Mayfield and Peony, her very dramatic white mare, have never seen any. Then a letter comes from the snow country. An old friend of Grandpa's has broken her leg, and her three animals are alone with two days of hay.
