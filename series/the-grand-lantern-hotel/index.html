The Grand Lantern Hotel

Spot-the-clue mysteries in a hotel where every corner is lit, because nothing can appear in a lit room.

by Clara Hollister Ages 8–12

Mystery Hides in Room 108

A Spooky Spot-the-Clue Chapter Book for Kids 8-12. Book 1 of The Grand Lantern Hotel.

Something in Room 108 is eating the guests' treasures. And something on the back stair is waiting for a wooden door to give. It's Lantern Week at the Grand Lantern Hotel, where every corner is lit, because nothing can appear in a lit room. Then a music disc vanishes from a guest's chest. A spyglass follows. Then a compass and a cookie disappear from a room whose door never opened.

Mystery Hides in Room 412

A Creepy Spot-the-Clue Chapter Book for Kids 8-12. Book 2 of The Grand Lantern Hotel.

Something with red eyes is sitting on the hotel roof. And only one boy knows who keeps leaving the door open. It's the last week of the river ferries at the Grand Lantern Hotel, where every corner is lit, because nothing can appear in a lit room. Then a ghost-hunter in Room 410 is knocked flat on the tower stair by something he can't see. All he saw were eyes. Two, then four, then six, then eight, with nothing around them at all.
