Howlwood Pack

A werewolf family guards its village every full moon, and Shep is really bad at holding still.

by Rhett Coldridge

Ages 8–12FunnyAction

For parents

Shep Howell, 11, comes from a werewolf family that quietly guards its village every full moon. The problem: Shep is really, really bad at holding still. Every book counts down the nights to the next full moon, with a Tail Check on how close he is to losing it.

Format
Chapter book: 20 to 26 short chapters, written for about a 3rd to 4th grade reading level.
How scary
Spooky: creepy moments, suspense and real danger, but nobody is hurt on the page.
Told by
Shep, in first person and present tense, so the action feels like it is happening right now.
The big idea
Self-control and loyalty: hold your own leash, and never leave a packmate behind.
At the back of each book
The Pack Code (one new rule per book) and Moon and Wolf Trivia.

A good fit for kids who have energy to burn, love wolves and dogs, and like action with plenty of laughs.

Book 1

Werewolf, Sit!

In the village of Mossrock, the most dangerous word is SIT. Especially on the night of the full moon.

Shep Howell is eleven, the second-fastest kid in town, and a werewolf. So are his mom and his little sister. Every full moon, the Howell pack slips into the deep spruce woods to chase skeletons away from the village, and nobody in Mossrock ever finds out. Then Mr. Pettibone announces the village's first-ever Moonlight Games, under "the brightest light there is." The full moon.

A Funny Wolf Pack Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.

Book 2

Werewolf, Shake!

It's the night of the full moon, and the moon isn't coming out.

Shep Howell is eleven, a werewolf, and really bad at holding still. Every full moon, his whole family turns into wolves and runs in the deep spruce woods. But this time the rain hides the moon, and the Turn gets stuck halfway. Ears. Tail. Nose. No wolf. All night long. And it's all happening at his best friend Hana's sleepover, in front of two friends who can't find out.

An Action-Packed Wolf Pack Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.

KindlePaperback

Book 3

Werewolf, Stay!

Four nights to full moon, and the biggest wolf in the family just walked down off the mountain.

Shep Howell is eleven, a werewolf, and really bad at holding still. So when Uncle Sten comes down from the snowy Grove for the first time ever, Shep is sure he has found his hero: a lone wolf who needs nobody and never has to sit. But a gang of armored skeletons is creeping uphill toward Uncle Sten's mountain. On the night of the full moon, Shep leaves his little sister behind and chases them all by himself, straight into the powder snow, where skeletons go in and something much worse comes out.

A Thrilling Winter Wolf Pack Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.
