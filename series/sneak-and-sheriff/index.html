Book 1

The Sneak Is at the Block Party

At Pinwheel Court's first real-life Sneak & Sheriff tournament, the prize vanishes at sunset. Then the string lights go out.

Every Friday night, Noemi Moreno and her friends squeeze onto one saggy garage couch to play their favorite hidden-role game. Every round, somebody is secretly the Sneak. Noemi is the best Sneak-catcher on the street, because she can't stand a lie. Now the block party has made the game real. The street's dented tin Badge disappears from under a glass cake dome while eleven players are sneaking around on purpose. Then the Court goes dark, and somebody walks through the night, tapping players out one by one.

A Suspenseful Secret-Role Whodunit Chapter Book with Logic Puzzles for Kids 8-12.
