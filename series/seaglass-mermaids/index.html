The Mermaid Who Hated Sand

A Funny Ocean Friendship Chapter Book for Kids 8-12. Book 1 of Seaglass Mermaids.

A mermaid who hates sand. A boy who won't put his face under water. And a treasure buried right where the sea meets the beach. Every night, grumbling little gurgles sneak through the fence of Gran's Axolotl Nursery. To keep the babies safe, eleven-year-old Prisma needs a heart of the sea for a brand-new conduit. There's just one problem. The treasure map's X sits on the beach, under a whole lot of sand, and sand is the worst thing in the ocean.

The Mermaid Who Raced a Nautilus

A Daring Ocean Friendship Chapter Book for Kids 8-12. Book 2 of Seaglass Mermaids.

A shore kid tames a nautilus so he can finally visit his best friend's underwater village. Getting there is the easy part. Ten-year-old Atlas Dela Cruz has about fifteen seconds of air, and Seaglass Reef is a thousand strokes away, on the far side of the dark Kelp Woods. So he makes a plan: catch three pufferfish, tame a nautilus, and ride it all the way to dinner with Prisma's mermaid family. One pufferfish in three works. Atlas has exactly three.
