The Hundred Sunrises

Three kids, one shared survival world and a hundred sunrises to reach together, where a player who is out still helps the team as the Watcher.

by Augustus Pell

Ages 8–12ActionAdventure

For parents

Nox, his big sister Asha and their neighbor Calla start a shared survival world with one life each and one goal: a hundred sunrises. A player who is out still helps the team as the Watcher.

Format
Chapter book: 20 to 26 short chapters, written for about a 3rd to 4th grade reading level.
How scary
Adventure, not spooky: real danger and narrow escapes, but no horror.
Told by
Nox, in first person, like the best 100-day recap ever.
The big idea
Everyone still counts: a team sticks together after a mistake.
At the back of each book
True facts about the real place behind each world, If You're Ever Lost Outside tips and a 100-day challenge card.

A good fit for kids who love Minecraft survival, 100-day challenges and teamwork.

Book 1

100 Days in the Frozen Woods

One life each. One hundred sunrises. No do-overs.

Nox, his big sister Asha, and Calla from next door start a brand-new world in Minecraft's Hardcore mode, where you get one life and the world never forgives. Their goal is to reach Sunrise 100 together. Their one rule: nobody plays the world alone. The frozen woods are beautiful and brutal. Zombies break wooden doors. Strays on the snowy plain shoot arrows that slow you down. A creeper can blow a hole in any wall.

A Nail-Biting Survival Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.

Kindle

Book 2

100 Days in the Rainforest

One life each. One hundred sunrises. One giant jungle.

Nox, his sister Asha, and Calla from next door start a new world in Minecraft's Hardcore mode, where you get one life and the world never forgives. This time they land in a rainforest so thick that the floor is dim at noon, so they build their home thirty blocks up a giant tree. Up high is safe from zombies. It is not safe from spiders. And one evening, with the roof almost done and the sun going down, one of the three keeps building because it's on the plan.

A Daring Survival Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.
