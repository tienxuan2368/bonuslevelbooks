Tornado Incoming!

A Thrilling Disaster-Science Chapter Book for Kids 8-12. Book 1 of The Lighthouse Lookouts.

In the game, you run out of the building. In real life, you get inside. Ingrid Storvik just mixed them up. Every Friday at 3:30, four friends log in from the back room of a small-town hardware store and drop onto Rumbletop Island, a disaster-survival game on Roblox. Ingrid has a plan for everything. Booker has a balloon and zero patience. Cyrus knows which roof will fall. Zora names every storm before it arrives.

Flash Flood Incoming!

A Fast-Paced Disaster-Science Chapter Book for Kids 8-12. Book 2 of The Lighthouse Lookouts.

In the game, you jump to beat the water. In real life, you turn around. Booker Ellis is about to learn the difference. Every Friday, four friends log in from the back room of a small-town hardware store and drop onto Rumbletop Island, a disaster-survival game on Roblox. Ingrid reads the sky. Cyrus knows which roof will hold. Zora names every disaster. And Booker jumps first, every single time.
