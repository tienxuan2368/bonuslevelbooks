Brush the dirt off an old find and walk into the day it was made: time-travel archaeology adventures.

by Jasper Tock Ages 9–12

Time Travel to the Age of First Fires

A Daring Archaeology Adventure for Kids 9-12. Book 1 of The Gate of Eight Clocks.

Brush the dirt off a broken pot, and you can walk into the day it was made. Elias Hourigan always jumps first and thinks on the way down. When he rushes a dig on Tower Hill, he loses a priceless find and crashes into a buried room, where a gate of black stone waits in the dark, ringed with eight gold clocks.
