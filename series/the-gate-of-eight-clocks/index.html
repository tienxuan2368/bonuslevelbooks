The Gate of Eight Clocks

Time-travel archaeology adventures with a kid who always jumps first, a gate ringed with eight gold clocks and real history.

by Jasper Tock

Ages 9–12Adventure

For parents

Three friends step through a gate ringed with eight gold clocks into real ages of the past, from the first fires to the first painted pottery. Every trip mixes time-travel adventure with real history and archaeology.

Format
Middle-grade novel: 24 to 30 chapters, written for about a 4th to 5th grade reading level.
How scary
Adventure, not spooky: real danger and narrow escapes, but no horror.
Told by
Third person, following Elias, Anjali and Pilar, with the trip into the past and the anchor at home told side by side.
The big idea
Learning from the past: every ruin was somebody's home.
At the back of each book
No quizzes: each book ends on the story, with a one-page teaser of the next trip.

A good fit for kids who love history, archaeology and big adventures.

Book 1

Time Travel to the Age of First Fires

Brush the dirt off a broken pot, and you can walk into the day it was made.

Elias Hourigan always jumps first and thinks on the way down. When he rushes a dig on Tower Hill, he loses a priceless find and crashes into a buried room, where a gate of black stone waits in the dark, ringed with eight gold clocks. Now the town wants to fill in the hill forever, and the vote is in two days. With careful Anjali, clock-keeping Pilar, and Cog the thieving fox, Elias sets an ancient pottery sherd in the gate's one empty frame.

A Daring Archaeology Adventure for Kids 9-12.

KindlePaperback

Book 2

Time Travel to the Age of Painted Pots

A heart pressed into ancient clay. A gate that opens only at sunset. One night to get home.

Anjali Rao hates guessing, and hates being wrong even more. For twenty-four days she has refused to set the Heartbreak sherd in the Gate of Eight Clocks, because she can't be sure which age it will open. When she finally steps through with Pilar, she finds Dialwood's own buried ruin alive: a ten-floor tower of painted clay, a candle in every window, and a town of potters on the night they break pots on purpose.

A Thrilling Archaeology Adventure for Kids 9-12.
