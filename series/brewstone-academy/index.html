The Potion That Took Forever

A Funny Witch Chapter Book for Kids 9-12. Book 1 of Brewstone Academy.

Every brew takes twenty seconds. Etta Honeycutt has never once waited for all twenty. At Brewstone Academy, a crooked stone tower between a sleepy village and a giggling swamp, all magic is real brewing and enchanting. Five days before Novice Test Day, Etta takes one shortcut too many. She skips a step, wastes her trio's only magma cream, and uses up the slimeball that kept her frog, Pestle, hopping at her heels.

The Potion That Healed a Zombie

A Daring Witch Chapter Book for Kids 9-12. Book 2 of Brewstone Academy.

Somebody keeps standing torches in the Gap. At Brewstone Academy, a crooked stone tower between a sleepy village and a giggling swamp, all magic is real brewing and enchanting. So when torches start appearing in the ring of bare floor around the enchanting table, the table turns cheap and weak, and everyone blames Etta Honeycutt, the most impatient Novice in the tower.
