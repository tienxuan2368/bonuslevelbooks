Brewstone Academy

Witch school in a crooked stone tower, where all magic is real brewing and one student never waits the full twenty seconds.

by Tilda Hartwell

Ages 9–12FunnyAdventure

For parents

Etta Honeycutt, 11, keeps a Brewing Log at a witch school in a crooked stone tower, where all magic is real potion brewing and she never waits the full twenty seconds. Recipe cards and spell notes sit between the scenes.

Format
Middle-grade novel: 24 to 30 chapters, written for about a 4th to 5th grade reading level.
How scary
Mostly adventure, with a few eerie moments. Fights are only ever against monsters, never people.
Told by
Etta, in first person, through the pages of her Brewing Log.
The big idea
Patience and practice, twenty seconds at a time.
At the back of each book
A Potion Ingredient Glossary and the Novice Exam, a three-round magic trivia quiz.

A good fit for kids who love magic schools, potions and funny diary-style books.

Book 1

The Potion That Took Forever

Every brew takes twenty seconds. Etta Honeycutt has never once waited for all twenty.

At Brewstone Academy, a crooked stone tower between a sleepy village and a giggling swamp, all magic is real brewing and enchanting. Five days before Novice Test Day, Etta takes one shortcut too many. She skips a step, wastes her trio's only magma cream, and uses up the slimeball that kept her frog, Pestle, hopping at her heels.

A Funny Witch Chapter Book for Kids 9-12.

Book 2

The Potion That Healed a Zombie

Somebody keeps standing torches in the Gap.

At Brewstone Academy, a crooked stone tower between a sleepy village and a giggling swamp, all magic is real brewing and enchanting. So when torches start appearing in the ring of bare floor around the enchanting table, the table turns cheap and weak, and everyone blames Etta Honeycutt, the most impatient Novice in the tower.
