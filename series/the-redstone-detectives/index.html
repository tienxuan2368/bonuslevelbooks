The Redstone Detectives

Solve-it-yourself mysteries with fair-play clues, a one-horned goat with opinions and a reveal readers can beat the detectives to.

by Hugo Brackett

Ages 8–12Mystery

Book 1

Detectives, Did the Fox Do It?

The whole town says the fox did it. The detectives say: prove it.

On the morning before Founders' Day, the treasure of Riverlatch is gone. The Founders' Heart has vanished from a locked hall in the night, and the stone pressure plate by the only door never clicked once. By sunrise, everyone blames Pockets, the fox who naps under Latch Bridge. Then a trail of redstone dust leads straight to the clubhouse of the Redstone Detectives, and Jory, Imani and Wynn become the suspects.

A Suspenseful Solve-It-Yourself Mystery Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.

Book 2

Detectives, Did the Snow Golem Do It?

Every gift on the mountain has turned to snow, and the whole town says the snow golem did it.

On the morning before Gift Eve, the twelve gift chests at the Whitecap Market Hall should be full of presents. Instead, eleven hold a single snowball each, and a trail of snow runs straight to Sergeant Snowbank, the snow golem who guards the door. If the gifts aren't back by dusk tomorrow, the Hall on the snowy slopes will close to the town for good.

A Thrilling Winter Solve-It-Yourself Mystery Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.
