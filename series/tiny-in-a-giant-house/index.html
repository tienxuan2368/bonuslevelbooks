Tiny Enough to Hide in a Teacup

A Spine-Tingling Hiding-Game Chapter Book for Kids 8-12. Book 1 of Tiny in a Giant House.

She finds everyone. He has never been found. Sol Benítez is the best seeker on Quince Street. Kenta Ward is the one hider she has never found: eleven Showdowns, not even a sleeve. Every Friday on Game Night, the two best friends log in to the Roblox game Hide and Seek Extreme and become tiny avatars in a giant house, where a spoon is a bridge, a cereal box is a skyscraper, and the It glows orange while it hunts.

Tiny Enough to Hide in a Stocking

A Spooky Christmas Hiding-Game Chapter Book for Kids 8-12. Book 2 of Tiny in a Giant House.

The game room closes forever at the Ding. The power keeps going out. And the seeker is now a jar of jam. Kenta Ward has never been found in a Showdown. Twelve times, Sol Benítez, the best seeker on Quince Street, has hunted him, and twelve times the clock has won. Tonight is a snow day and the last night of a winter event in the Roblox game Hide and Seek Extreme.
