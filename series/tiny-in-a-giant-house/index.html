Ages 8–12SpookyAction

Book 1

Tiny Enough to Hide in a Teacup

She finds everyone. He has never been found.

Sol Benítez is the best seeker on Quince Street. Kenta Ward is the one hider she has never found: eleven Showdowns, not even a sleeve. Every Friday on Game Night, the two best friends log in to the Roblox game Hide and Seek Extreme and become tiny avatars in a giant house, where a spoon is a bridge, a cereal box is a skyscraper, and the It glows orange while it hunts.

A Spine-Tingling Hiding-Game Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.

Book 2

Tiny Enough to Hide in a Stocking

The game room closes forever at the Ding. The power keeps going out. And the seeker is now a jar of jam.

Kenta Ward has never been found in a Showdown. Twelve times, Sol Benítez, the best seeker on Quince Street, has hunted him, and twelve times the clock has won. Tonight is a snow day and the last night of a winter event in the Roblox game Hide and Seek Extreme. The Holiday Living Room closes at 7:45: a giant tree full of orange bulbs that hide the seeker's glow, a toy train that whistles every twenty seconds, and five red stockings on the mantel.

A Spooky Christmas Hiding-Game Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.
