The Gloamford Sightings

Four friends in a foggy mining village check every rumor clue by clue: creepy campfire mysteries about truth versus gossip, with no gore and nobody hurt.

by Casper Vellum

Ages 10–14SpookyMystery

Book 1

Herobrine Was Seen in the Fog

Two pale eyes on the ridge. They never blink. And every night, they are watching the ferry.

In the foggy mining village of Gloamford, everybody knows the legend of Herobrine. So when Ozzie Batista sees two pale lights on the North Ridge at dusk, he writes it on the village Board and signs his name. By breakfast, Herobrine has a beard. By Fire Night, he has stolen a sheep.

A Spooky Campfire Mystery for Kids 10-14.

KindlePaperback

Book 2

Herobrine Was Seen in the Old Deepworks

A square tunnel opened under the mountain overnight. Nobody dug it. And something tall was standing at the end of it.

In the snowbound mining village of Gloamford, nobody goes past the Last Torch. So when young miner Jowan Kitto swears he saw Herobrine in the Old Deepworks, Petra Halvorsen, the girl who checks everything, tells the whole village it was nothing: a shadow and a noise. By sundown, her "nothing" is a rumor with her name on it. By dusk, Jowan has gone back down with eight torches to prove her wrong. He doesn't come home.

A Creepy Campfire Mystery for Kids 10-14.
