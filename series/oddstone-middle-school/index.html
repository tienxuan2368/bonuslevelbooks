Monster Homeroom and the Midnight Bell

A Funny Monster School Mystery for Kids 9-12. Book 1 of Oddstone Middle School.

At a school where nobody is normal, the normal kid is the weird one. Lexi Plainfield is eleven, brand-new in town, and completely, totally normal. Everyone else in Homeroom 6-M has a thing. Pearl turns see-through when she's embarrassed. Rufus sinks like a stone and always has something useful in his pockets. Basil sparkles purple when he thinks hard and giggles at the worst possible moments.

Monster Homeroom and the Vanishing Pumpkins

A Funny Monster School Spooky Mystery for Kids 9-12. Book 2 of Oddstone Middle School.

Somebody is stealing the pumpkins. Except it isn't somebody. It's something. Pearl Wispington is ghost-folk, a drama club star and the lead in the fall play at the Oddstone Playhouse. There's one problem. When she's nervous, she fades. When she stands near a soul lantern, she glows like a glow squid. So Pearl has a secret: a week before opening night, she quietly had the lanterns taken down from the dark space above the stage.
