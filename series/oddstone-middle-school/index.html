Ages 9–12FunnyMysterySpooky

Book 1

Monster Homeroom and the Midnight Bell

At a school where nobody is normal, the normal kid is the weird one.

Lexi Plainfield is eleven, brand-new in town, and completely, totally normal. Everyone else in Homeroom 6-M has a thing. Pearl turns see-through when she's embarrassed. Rufus sinks like a stone and always has something useful in his pockets. Basil sparkles purple when he thinks hard and giggles at the worst possible moments. So on her very first morning, Lexi panics and tells the whole class she's witch-folk. On her aunt's side. She doesn't have an aunt.

A Funny Monster School Mystery for Kids 9-12.

Book 2

Monster Homeroom and the Vanishing Pumpkins

Somebody is stealing the pumpkins. Except it isn't somebody. It's something.

Pearl Wispington is ghost-folk, a drama club star and the lead in the fall play at the Oddstone Playhouse. There's one problem. When she's nervous, she fades. When she stands near a soul lantern, she glows like a glow squid. So Pearl has a secret: a week before opening night, she quietly had the lanterns taken down from the dark space above the stage.

A Funny Monster School Spooky Mystery for Kids 9-12.
