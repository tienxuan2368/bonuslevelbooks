Pizza, Hold the Panic

A Funny Pizza Chapter Book for Kids 8-12. Book 1 of The Extra Cheese Crew.

Hi! Welcome! Everything is fine! (It is not fine.) Ten-year-old Mae Galanis isn't allowed behind the counter of her family's real pizza shop until she's fourteen. So after school, in the last red booth of Galanis House of Pizza, she finally gets her own username and clocks in to her friends' favorite pizza game. She has a fourteen-step plan to be the best new hire in history, and every step says "by myself."

Pizza, Hold the Smoke

A Rush-Hour Pizza Chapter Book for Kids 8-12. Book 2 of The Extra Cheese Crew.

Fire in the kitchen! (Again.) (It wasn't Emilio.) (Probably.) Eleven days into her job at a pizza game, ten-year-old Mae Galanis knows the oldest rule in the Pizza Place: nobody gets paid until the pizza reaches the door. Now her best friend Emilio, who treats every pizza like a painting, has challenged her to a cook-off during the Golden Rush, the best four minutes of the day.
