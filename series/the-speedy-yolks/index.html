The Speedy Yolks

A gaming squad that has never pulled off a big egg steal. Not once. Until now.

by Benny Hatchwell

Ages 8–12FunnyAction

Book 1

Grab the Goose Egg

Sneak past the giant goose. Grab the egg. RUN.

Ten-year-old Zeke Delgado is the fastest runner on his gaming squad, the Speedy Yolks, and the loudest. There's one problem. In the whole history of the team, they have pulled off exactly zero big steals. Then a giant, glowing egg appears in Duchess the goose's nest. It's the rarest egg on the server. It's also so heavy that nobody can outrun the goose while carrying it.

A Funny Gamer-Squad Adventure for Kids 8-12.

KindlePaperback

Book 2

Grab the Icicle Egg

One giant ox. One icy bridge. Three black banners waiting in the dark.

It's update day, and a brand-new zone called Shiver Ridge has opened. Its guardian, Big Shaggy, is a musk ox the size of a hill, and he can out-run anyone who carries his egg. Even the server's fastest player. Ten-year-old Zeke Delgado and his gaming squad, the Speedy Yolks, go in anyway. His big sister Abril has a plan for every step. His best friend Kiyo knows every number. Abuela Toña, a retired umpire, has the slowest speed on the server and the loudest "SAFE!" in the kitchen.

An Action-Packed Winter Gamer-Squad Adventure for Kids 8-12.
