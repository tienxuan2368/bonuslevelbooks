Bacon Hair Rises

The kid everyone calls bacon hair keeps saving the day, and learns that what you wear never decides what you're worth.

by Lyle Brindlewood

Ages 8–12FunnyAction

For parents

Nolan Tibbs, 11, is the only kid in town still wearing the plain starter look, and everyone calls him bacon hair. He keeps saving the day anyway.

Format
Chapter book: 20 to 26 short chapters, written for about a 3rd to 4th grade reading level.
How scary
Adventure, not spooky: real danger and narrow escapes, but no horror.
Told by
Nolan, in first person, with a wisecrack for every situation.
The big idea
Self-worth: what you wear, or buy, never decides what you're worth.
At the back of each book
Free Glow-Up Ideas that cost nothing, and the Spawnbury Silly Awards.

A good fit for kids who love underdog stories, avatar games and a lot of jokes.

Book 1

Bacon Hair Saves the Holidays

Everyone in Spawnbury thinks Nolan Tibbs just got here. He has lived there his whole life.

Nolan is the only kid in town who still wears the Starter Look: plain shirt, plain pants and brown hair in three flat strips that folks call bacon hair. His family runs the Welcome Hut beside the Spawn Pad. In four days it's Christmas Eve, the night more fresh spawns arrive than any other. Then the Spawn Pad cracks and goes dark, right under Nolan's shoes.

A Funny Christmas Underdog Chapter Book About Self-Esteem for Kids 8-12.

Book 2

Bacon Hair Saves the Kart Cup

In Kart Canyon, every kart has the same top speed. Nobody told the kids who won't pick Nolan Tibbs for a team.

Nolan has lived in Spawnbury his whole life, but he still wears the plain Starter Look every newcomer arrives in: brown hair in three flat strips that folks call bacon hair. So when Cup Week starts, nobody wants him in their kart. Except his best friend, Dalia, who has never driven one either. Their first kart falls apart on the first ramp. A lazy visitor named Loosebolt bends the canyon's rules to watch things fall: welds come loose, warnings vanish, and karts get stuck in the lava.

A Daring Underdog Chapter Book About Self-Esteem for Kids 8-12.
