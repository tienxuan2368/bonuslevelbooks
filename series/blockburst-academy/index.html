Blockburst Academy

The lowest-ranked squad at a superhero school, where every power works like something from the blocky world.

by Dexter Halberd

Ages 8–12FunnyAction

Book 1

Superhero Rookies and the Big Fizzle

The Fizzles are ranked dead last. Tonight, they're the only heroes left.

Rafi Okafor is ten, and his brand-new superpower is… wind. At Blockburst Academy, every student's power works exactly like something real in the blocky world: a wind charge, a Swiftness potion, Invisibility, the Turtle Master's tough shell. On his very first day, Rafi's gust wrecks the Showcase, and his squad lands at the bottom of the Hero Board with a nickname nobody wants: the Fizzles.

A Funny Superpower School Chapter Book for Ages 8-12.

Book 2

Superhero Rookies and the Glide-Off

Somebody swapped the star racer's rockets. Now the Fizzles are the only heroes close enough to bring him home.

At Blockburst Academy, every student's superpower works exactly like something real in the blocky world. Rafi Okafor's power is wind: four puffs, and then he needs a snack. His squad, the Fizzles, is ranked dead last. So at the Glide-Off, a sky race around Cloudcap Peak, they get the worst job on the mountain: crewing for Beckett Rowe, the show-off flier who gave them their nickname.

An Action-Packed Superpower School Chapter Book for Ages 8-12.
