Book 1

Climbing the Zap-Brick Tower

Fall off, float down, try again. That's how the Tower works.

In the toy-bright town of Toehold stands the Tower: the tallest, brightest, wobbliest tower anywhere. Every few minutes it shuffles into a brand-new climb. There are no halfway spots. One toe on a red zap brick, and you float all the way back down to the pink Soft Spot. Today is Climb Day. Everyone who reaches the Top before the last BONG gets their name on the Top Wall, and Rio Asante wants his name up there more than anything. There's just one problem.

A Funny Keep-Trying Chapter Book for Kids 6-8.
