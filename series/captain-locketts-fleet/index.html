Captain Lockett's Fleet

An eleven-year-old captain, a ship that never moves and a little fleet of boats chasing real treasure across the bay, with a first mate who corrects every log entry.

by Jonah Saltwell

Ages 8–12FunnyAction

For parents

Captain Dylan Lockett has a crew, a flag and a parrot who outranks him. With his blunt first mate Renata, his builder friend Ravi and his quiet little sister Effie, he sails a fleet of small boats out of Firstlight Harbor after shipwrecks and buried treasure, with real danger at sea and plenty of laughs.

Format
Chapter book: 20 to 26 short chapters, written for about a 3rd to 4th grade reading level.
How scary
Adventure, not spooky: real danger and narrow escapes, but no horror.
Told by
Dylan himself, in first person, with grand Captain's Log entries and a one-line "Correction" from Renata underneath.
The big idea
Loyalty: nobody sails alone, and nobody gets left on the beach.
At the back of each book
The crew's Pirate Code with a write-your-own-code prompt, a Treasure Map Puzzle and a short note for grown-ups.

A good fit for kids who love pirates, treasure maps and funny best-friend crews.

Treasure Hunt in the Shipwreck

Captain Dylan Lockett has a crew, a flag, a parrot who outranks him, and a ship that has never moved an inch.

So when a dolphin leads his little fleet of boats to a shipwreck lying on its side, and his shipwright swims up with a real treasure map, Dylan knows this is it: the find that will ring the bell on Harbor Day. There is just one problem. Captain Cordelia Prowse, the fanciest captain in the bay, saw the map too.
