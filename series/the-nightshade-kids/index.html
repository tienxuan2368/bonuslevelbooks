The Nightshade Kids

A family of night folk, a nosy neighbor and a lot of sun-sneezes: spooky-funny stories about being proud of who you are.

by Vivienne Darrow

Ages 8–12FunnySpooky

For parents

Mina Nightshade is eleven, a vampire, and trying very hard to seem normal in a blocky little village. Each book is a spooky-funny family adventure, with Mina's numbered Rules for Passing as Normal and cryptic notes from her grandmother along the way.

Format
Chapter book: 20 to 26 short chapters, written for about a 3rd to 4th grade reading level.
How scary
Spooky: creepy moments, suspense and real danger, but nobody is hurt on the page.
Told by
Mina herself, in first person, with a dry, deadpan sense of humor.
The big idea
Be proud of who you are, and let your family be a little weird.
At the back of each book
Mina's Vampire Dictionary, a Spooky Would You Rather game and a short note for grown-ups.

A good fit for kids who love Minecraft nights, funny narrators and spooky family comedies.

Book 1

Vampires Are Bad at Being Normal

Mina Nightshade has one goal this week: be normal. That is very hard when you are an eleven-year-old vampire.

The mayor of Hollowbrook has announced the village's first-ever Sunrise Parade. Every family must spend four nights building a parade arch, then walk to the lake at dawn and take off their hats for the first sunbeam. For the Nightshades, who sneeze little puffs of smoke in the sun and hide iron helmets inside their hats, this is a disaster. And they have been given the place of honor, facing the sunrise.

A Funny Family Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.

Book 2

Vampires Are Bad at Houseguests

Kofi Mensah has a plan. He is staying with his best friend's family for five nights, and by the end of the week, he is going to be a vampire.

There is just one problem. Mina Nightshade's family is night folk, and night folk are born, not made. You can't catch it. You can't practice it. And you definitely can't fake a sun-sneeze with a pinch of flour, no matter what the nosy neighbor collects in her bottles. But Kofi tries everything. Beetroot soup for breakfast. Bat lessons with Mina's little brother, who keeps turning into a chicken. A cape that used to be a banner. And no sleep at all, because night folk don't sleep at night.

A Spooky-Funny Family Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.

KindlePaperback

Book 3

Vampires Are Bad at School Plays

Mina Nightshade told her little brother to be quiet. Just once. Then he turned into a bat.

For years, seven-year-old Barney has jumped off the library ladder shouting "BAT TIME!" and landed in a puff of chicken feathers. But two nights before the school play, he jumps without a sound, and this time it works. Now the loudest kid in Hollowbrook is a small brown bat who flies away from anyone who comes near. And he can't turn back.

A Spine-Tingling Family Chapter Book for Kids 8-12.
