Book gifts for kids who love Minecraft and Roblox

Updated November 7, 2026 · By the Bonus Level Books team · Save to Pinterest

Every December it's the same question: what do you get a kid who only wants game stuff? A book set in a world they already love is a gift that lasts past the first week, and it gets them reading over the break.

Here are our gift picks by age. They're unofficial chapter books with original characters, every book is a complete story, and most come as paperbacks that wrap nicely and fit in a stocking.

Gift tips

Paperbacks are the easiest to wrap and the best fit for a stocking. Order early in December, because shipping slows down right before Christmas. If your reader has a Kindle, the Kindle edition is the instant option.

Questions parents ask

Are these official Minecraft or Roblox books?

No. They are unofficial stories with original characters. They are not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Mojang, Microsoft, Roblox Corporation or any game's creators.

Which book should I start with?

Any book. Every book is a complete adventure, so book 2 works fine as a first read. If you're giving a set, start with book 1.

Will my child like it if they don't play the game?

Yes. Each story explains what a reader needs to know as it goes.
