Spooky chapter books for 8-year-olds, from silly to shivery

Updated November 7, 2026 · By the Bonus Level Books team

Lots of eight-year-olds love a good scare, as long as it's the fun kind. Our spooky series are scary in the campfire-story sense: creaks, shadows and things that go bump, with nobody hurt on the page and no gore, ever.

They're sorted here from silliest to shiveriest, so you can match the book to your reader.

Tips for a scared-but-happy reader

Read the first chapter together in daylight. Talk about what's real and what's story. Let them stop and come back later, since a good scary book is still good tomorrow. And if a story keeps them up at night, switch to a funny series for a while. There's no prize for pushing through.

Questions parents ask

Are these books too scary for an 8-year-old?

Most aren't. Everything in the first three groups is written for ages 6 to 12 and stays on the fun side of scary. Only The Village of Borrowed Voices is written for ages 10 to 14.

Is there gore or violence?

No. Nobody is hurt on the page, and game weapons are only ever used on monsters.

Are they official game books?

No. They are unofficial stories with original characters, not affiliated with or endorsed by any game's makers.
