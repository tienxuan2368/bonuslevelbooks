Roblox books for kids: chapter books for fans

Updated November 7, 2026 · By the Bonus Level Books team · Save to Pinterest

Plenty of Roblox books teach coding. These are stories: chapter books set in the kinds of games Roblox fans play every day, with original characters, real stakes and short chapters that end on a hook.

They are unofficial and written for fans. Each book is a complete adventure, so your reader can start anywhere.

Online safety, built into the stories

Many of these stories happen inside a game the characters play with friends. The good habits are part of the plot: playing with friends they know in real life, keeping voice chat off, being a good sport and telling a grown-up when something feels wrong. That makes the books an easy way to start the same conversation at home.

Questions parents ask

Are these official Roblox books?

No. They are unofficial fan fiction with original characters. They are not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Roblox Corporation or any game's creators.

What ages are they for?

Team Checkpoint and Floor by Floor are for ages 6 to 8. The Village of Borrowed Voices is for ages 10 to 14. Every other series here is for ages 8 to 12.

Does my child need to play Roblox to enjoy them?

No. Each story explains the game as it goes.

Do the series have to be read in order?

No. Every book is a complete adventure.
