Flee the Snowbound Observatory

A Spine-Tingling Hack-and-Escape Chapter Book for Kids 8-12

by Calvin Drummond

Ages 8–12Level 2ActionMystery
New & finding readers
KindlePaperback

No lamp. No hum. No music. Just a door snapping open somewhere in the dark.

Snow has closed every school, and a brand-new map has dropped: the Snowbound Observatory, a mountaintop maze of telescopes, star theaters and frost pods. The Thaw Crew has three snow days to flee it together.

Then a player named PinDrop joins the server. It never waves. It never types a word. And when the game makes it the Chiller, it picks Hush, the silent trick: no light, no sound, only frost creeping up the glass while your friends wait for rescue.

Anika is the crew's navigator. She always knows where the Chiller is. Except now she doesn't, and her guesses keep getting her friends frozen. To win a Full Thaw on Hush, she'll have to say three hard words out loud, "I don't know," and trust Grace, Wilder and little Tolu to be her eyes and ears.

For parents

Format
Chapter book: 20 to 26 short chapters, written for about a 3rd to 4th grade reading level.
How scary
Spooky: creepy moments, suspense and real danger, but nobody is hurt on the page.
At the back of the book
Code-breaker puzzles with hints, plus an answer key that explains how each code works.

A good fit for kids who love escape rooms, codes and heist movies.
