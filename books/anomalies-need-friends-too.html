Night Shift at Pawprint Hospital · Book 3 of 3

Anomalies Need Friends Too

A Creepy and Heartfelt Night-Shift Chapter Book for Kids 8-12

by Fern Wickersham

Ages 8–12Level 2SpookyAnimals
KindlePaperback

At 10:10 PM, a kitten rings the bell. The camera says nobody is there.

Every night at seven, Abe Tesfaye and his best friends Dulce and Tavo clock in to Pawprint Hospital, their private server in a spooky night-shift animal-hospital game on Roblox. Dulce works the Night Window. Tavo runs and names every patient. Abe runs the cameras, and Abe never misses anything.

Until the night he does.

At a stranger's hospital, the game's fastest player shutters a cinnamon kitten without a second look. Then her meow comes back from every room someone has just left. The cameras show patients who already went home. And a copy slips inside, because Abe called it clean.

The next night, she is waiting on Pawprint's front step.

Every hour, she comes closer. On the cameras, something gray stands far down the hall, nearer every time Abe checks. At home, the Tesfayes' shy shelter cat won't come out from under the guest bed, however carefully Abe watches.

Who is the kitten waiting for?

For parents

A good fit for kids who love animals, vets and spooky-but-kind mysteries.
