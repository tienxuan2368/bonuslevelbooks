Every racer on the server has a ride. Except the squad's own runner.

Ten-year-old Zeke Delgado is the fastest kid on the Speedy Yolks, until everybody else gets a ride and he's left on foot. Then he tells the server's biggest show-off, in capital letters, that he'll race him on Saturday. There's only one problem. The only pet in Zeke's pen is Crouton, and Crouton is a very small pigeon.

Zeke's plan: grab the Rocket Egg, a Legendary egg guarded by a giant parrot who copies every sound and flies straight across the jungle while you run the long way around. Hatch something fast. Win.

But the parrot keeps winning, a rival keeps waiting for every catch, and when the Rocket Egg finally drops at Zeke's feet, it belongs to the one player on the server who has never had a big egg at all.

Read the opening pages

EGGS: 0 — RACE NIGHT: 5 DAYS

ROLL the tape. Saturday night, the last race of the night on our server. That’s me at the starting line. ZoomYolk, the runner of the Speedy Yolks.

I’m sitting on a pigeon.

It’s my pigeon, Crouton, and he is about the size of a sandwich. My sneakers almost touch the ground on both sides of him. At the end of the line, MegaYoink is sitting on a purple ride as big as a car.

Freeze it there. Now rewind five days, because you need to know how a kid ends up racing on a sandwich.

On Monday the fog came in and didn’t leave. When I looked out the kitchen window, our backyard was gone. The swing set was a gray shadow. The bird feeder was a rumor.

“Taco Monday,” said Mom, and set the Tomato on the counter.

The Tomato is a plastic tomato with a timer inside, and it has never lost an argument. Monday’s session is the long one, a whole hour. Mom cranked it around until it clicked.

Tick, tick, tick.

On Mondays Kiyo’s mom works late, so he eats at our table. He lives next door and has a number for everything. He was squaring up his index cards beside the old laptop, a card for each pet on our server.

My big sister, Abril, had her binder open. She is twelve and plans everything, usually in five colors.

Abuela sat at the end of the table with her tablet propped on the fruit bowl. Her glasses hung on their beaded chain, and she was crunching a pickle. She spent thirty years calling kids’ baseball games, and she still calls everything.

And I’m Zeke. Ten years old, one sock, and the runner on our team. I’ve spent more hours on our treadmill than anybody on the squad. I’m the fast one.

I was the fast one, anyway. Then last week, everybody else got a ride.

Here’s how you get a ride in our game. On some nights, stars fall. Stay on the server from sunrise in the game, and one of them comes down for you. Carry it home, give it to one of the pets in your pen, and that pet is a ride for good.

Abuela got hers first. Her gosling, Zero, carries her up and down Base Row now, flapping and honking. Abril’s star turned Skillet, her shaggy musk-ox calf, into a ride. Kiyo’s turned Gravy, his little rock grump, into one.

My stars kept landing in terrible places. The top of a volcano. The far end of the Crispy Dunes.

Every half hour, our server has a race. Everybody lines up across the lobby square on their rides. Then it’s out to the Leafy Loop, once around the jungle, and back to the line. The top three get trophies, and you can spend trophies on boosters and stuff.
