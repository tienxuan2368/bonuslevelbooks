My Horse Thinks She's a Snowplow

A Brave Winter Horse Chapter Book for Girls Ages 8-12

by Maisie Saddleton

Ages 8–12Level 2AdventureAnimals
KindlePaperback

My horse thinks she's a snowplow. The snow doesn't agree. Neither do the things that come out of it at night.

Snow never falls on Sunny Meadow Ranch, so ten-year-old Tansy Mayfield and Peony, her very dramatic white mare, have never seen any. Then a letter comes from the snow country. An old friend of Grandpa's has broken her leg, and her three animals are alone with two days of hay.

So Tansy, her mom and her best friend Ezra ride north to bring them home. Up there, the white ground lies. Powder snow looks like ordinary snow, but anything that steps in it sinks, and anything without leather freezes. At night, strays walk out of the dark with arrows that make your legs slow as honey. And when an arrow snaps the lead of a frightened young mare, Tansy must walk onto the white in her everyday boots, while Ezra, who has never dared to ride a horse, climbs onto the tallest one in the snow country.

For parents

Format
Chapter book: 20 to 26 short chapters, written for about a 3rd to 4th grade reading level.
How scary
Adventure, not spooky: real danger and narrow escapes, but no horror.
At the back of the book
Real Horse Care Facts (each checked in two sources) and a Name Your Horse! game.

A good fit for kids who love horses, animals and farm life.
